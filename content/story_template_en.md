# NABZ Mysteries: approved six-beat Short structure

Use this structure for every new mystery Short, but never for film-promo reels.

1. Hook: one verified contrast, no more than 14 words. No intro, date, hype, or setup.
2. Main question: one clear mystery, with only the context needed to understand it.
3. Clue: one concrete detail that lets the viewer form a first guess.
4. Turn: a new verified fact that corrects or complicates that guess. Vary the opening: But, Yet, The problem was, Only one thing did not fit, Then, and so on.
5. Discovery: separate proven facts, the strongest explanation, and what remains unknown. Never fill gaps with sensational guesses.
6. Ending: reinterpret the hook without repeating it. Rotate endings: a new meaning for the opening image, one hard fact, the boundary of what is known, or a restrained contrast.

Quality gate: exactly 6 beats; 50-95 total words; beat 1 <= 14 words; every beat has text and visual; one question only; no unsupported hype; facts and theories clearly separated; ending adds meaning instead of repeating the hook.

Pacing: target 40-50 seconds with natural narration. Do not speed narration to force a fit.

Film-promo reels remain on their separate film structure and CTA.
