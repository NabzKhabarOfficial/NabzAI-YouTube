One passenger walked off a ship. Two hundred eleven stayed, and vanished.
July 1909. The liner Waratah left Durban for Cape Town. Where did she go?
A passenger named Claude Sawyer got off early. He said she rolled badly and felt wrong.
The next morning another ship exchanged signals with her. She had no radio, so that was the last contact.
No wreck, bodies or lifeboats were found. Probable: a giant wave capsized her. Searches found the wrong wreck.
The one man who walked away is still the best witness to her final days.
