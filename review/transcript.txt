A woman started dancing in the street, and for days she could not stop.
Strasbourg, 1518. Within weeks, dozens joined her. What made them dance?
Doctors called it an illness of hot blood, and the cure was more dancing. So the city hired musicians.
But with music, the dancers multiplied. A sickness of the body does not spread by watching.
Proven: records describe the outbreak. Probable: mass panic, in hungry people who feared a dancing curse. Unknown: the exact cause.
And the musicians hired to cure it probably made the fire burn hotter.
