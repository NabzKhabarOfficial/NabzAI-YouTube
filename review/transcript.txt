Six people were murdered on a farm, but someone stayed there for days.
Bavaria, 1922. Who killed the family at Hinterkaifeck?
Before the murders, the farmer saw footprints in the snow leading in, and none leading out. Maybe someone was hiding.
One thing did not fit a robbery: the family's money was left untouched.
Proven: after the killings, someone fed the animals and smoke rose from the chimney. Probable: the killer hid in the house. Unknown: who.
Those footprints went only one way, perhaps because their owner never meant to leave.
