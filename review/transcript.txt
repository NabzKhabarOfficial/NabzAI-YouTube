Three lighthouse keepers vanished from a tiny island, and one of them left his coat behind.
Scotland, December 1900. The Flannan Isles light went dark.
Inside, the lamp was clean. Beds made. Nothing out of place.
Outside, iron railings were twisted, equipment ripped away by the sea.
A giant wave, they said. But why were all three outside at once, in winter?
