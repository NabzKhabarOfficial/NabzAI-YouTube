Three prisoners left America’s most famous island prison, but never left a confirmed trace.
Did Frank Morris and the Anglin brothers survive the cold waters after escaping in 1962?
They built dummy heads, dug through cell walls, and launched a raft made from raincoats.
The raft remnants and personal effects were found, but no bodies ever surfaced.
Proven: the three escaped the cellhouse. Probable: they drowned in the bay. Unknown: whether any reached land and disappeared.
Alcatraz was designed to make escape impossible; the unanswered question is whether the water finished the job.
