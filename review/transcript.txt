A real prospector became the name of a mine no one can locate.
Did Jacob Waltz truly hide a rich mine in Arizona’s Superstition Mountains?
Records place Waltz in Arizona and show treasure seekers searching soon after his 1891 death.
But maps, Peralta stories, deathbed clues, and gold sightings shift from version to version.
Proven: Waltz existed, and the region has mining history. Probable: the legend combines real prospecting with later storytelling. Unknown: any lost bonanza mine.
The enduring treasure may be the story’s ability to turn missing evidence into a destination.
