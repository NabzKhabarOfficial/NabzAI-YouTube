A jury named a British lord as a killer. He was already gone.
London, 1974. The nanny was killed at his wife's home. So where did Lord Lucan go?
His wife said he attacked her too. A year later, an inquest jury named him the killer.
But his borrowed car had been found near the port of Newhaven, with blood inside.
For decades people claimed to see him abroad. None was confirmed. Many think he died that week.
He was declared dead in 2016. The car by the sea is still his last known address.
