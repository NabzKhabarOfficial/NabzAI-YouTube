A small clay disc carries a careful message that still has no agreed reading.
Is it a Minoan text, a ritual object, or a later forgery?
Found at Phaistos in 1908, it has 45 signs stamped into a spiral.
Its corrections and archaeological context support authenticity, but the text has no close parallel.
Proven: the disc exists; probable: it records a writing system; unknown: its language and message.
The missing key is not hidden in the spiral—it may be another disc, never found.
