A royal mystery changed when another king was found under a car park.
What happened to Edward V and Richard after entering the Tower?
They disappeared from public view in 1483; two child skeletons found in 1674 were never conclusively identified.
Richard III’s remains were identified through archaeology and DNA, but that solved another mystery.
Proven: the princes vanished. Probable: historians widely suspect murder. Unknown: who ordered it and where they are.
Finding one missing king clarified a separate grave, leaving the Tower silent.
