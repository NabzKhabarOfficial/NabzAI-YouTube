Two warships entered the Arctic; their wrecks waited underwater for a century.
What happened to the 129 people who sailed toward the Northwest Passage in 1845?
A message found on King William Island said the ships were trapped, then abandoned in 1848.
The mystery changed when Inuit knowledge helped guide modern searches to the seafloor.
Proven: Erebus was found in 2014 and Terror in 2016. Probable: ice, illness, and starvation destroyed the expedition. Unknown: each person’s final path.
The ships were not erased; they were preserved, quietly holding the last chapter beneath Arctic water.
