Something exploded over Siberia, a thousand times stronger than Hiroshima, and left no crater.
June 1908. A blast that big should leave a crater.
Eighty million trees lay flat, all pointing outward, like spokes.
But at the center, trees still stood, stripped bare.
So it burst in the air: likely a space rock, kilometres up.
That is why there is no crater. It never landed.
