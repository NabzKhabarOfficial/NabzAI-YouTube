An ancient historian described an army disappearing between two Egyptian oases.
Did a sandstorm bury Cambyses’ soldiers, or did the story grow in transit?
Herodotus says a Persian force left Thebes, reached an oasis, then vanished in the desert.
Later claims of bones and Persian objects near Siwa were never securely documented or accepted as proof.
Proven: Herodotus recorded the disappearance. Probable: a smaller force may have been lost in desert conditions. Unknown: its size, route, and fate.
The desert keeps the story alive because one ancient report still has no matching excavation.
