Two well-dressed men were found dead on a hill, wearing lead eye masks.
Brazil, 1966. Two electronics technicians, and no wounds. What killed them?
A note beside them said: take the capsules, then wait for the signal. Maybe a drug.
But no capsules were found, and doctors could not find a cause of death.
Proven: the men, the masks and the note were real. Probable: a ritual or experiment went wrong. Unknown: what killed them.
Their death is certain, and so is their belief. Only the signal they waited for is missing.
