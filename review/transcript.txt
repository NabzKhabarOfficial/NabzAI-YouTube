Cameras saw him walk into a bar. None saw him leave.
Ohio, 2006. Medical student Brian Shaffer went out with friends. How do you leave a bar unseen?
Footage shows him arriving after one in the morning, then talking with two women near closing time.
The problem was the exits. Every camera was checked, and he never appears.
His car stayed parked. Police believe he left by a route the cameras missed. Where he went is unknown.
The cameras did their job. They only showed where he was not.
