A killer mailed coded letters to newspapers. One took fifty-one years to crack.
California, late 1960s. He called himself the Zodiac. Who was he?
A couple cracked his first code within days. Maybe it would reveal his name.
But in that code, he wrote he would not give his name. The second, solved in 2020, had none either.
Proven: at least five murders. Probable: the letters came from the killer. Unknown: his identity.
His long codes were opened; the shortest one, which might hold his name, is still locked.
