Lights keep appearing in one Norwegian valley, even after instruments joined the search.
What produces the recurring lights reported in Hessdalen?
Project Hessdalen recorded sightings, photographs, radar returns, and magnetic measurements, especially during its 1984 field study.
Sensors made the phenomenon measurable, but measurements did not identify one cause; ideas include plasma, geology, and ball lightning.
Proven: unusual luminous events are repeatedly observed and instrumented. Probable: natural atmospheric or geological processes may contribute. Unknown: a complete mechanism.
The mystery is no longer whether observers saw lights, but what the valley is making visible.
