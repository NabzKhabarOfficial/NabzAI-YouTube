One calm bystander may have filmed history, then disappeared from the record.
Who was the scarfed woman holding a camera during Kennedy’s assassination?
She appears in several films and photographs near Dealey Plaza’s motorcade, but no confirmed film from her camera surfaced.
Beverly Oliver later claimed the role, yet her age, camera timeline, and story were challenged.
Proven: an unidentified woman appears in the footage. Probable: she was an ordinary spectator. Unknown: her name and whether she recorded anything.
Her mystery survives because the images preserve her presence while withholding her identity.
