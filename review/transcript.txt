A burned body was found in Norway, surrounded by carefully erased clues.
Who was the Isdal woman, and why had she traveled under so many names?
Police found suitcases, wigs, removed labels, and a travel note linked to several hotels.
The note recorded journeys, not a confirmed spy message.
Proven: she used aliases and died after sedatives and fire. Probable: police favored suicide. Unknown: her name, motive, and any other person’s role.
Her careful disguise concealed a life, not a final answer; even the clues still refuse to introduce her.
