Two centuries of digging found obstacles, but no confirmed treasure.
What, if anything, was placed beneath Oak Island?
Searchers reported timber platforms, flooding, coconut fiber, and repeated shafts near a depression.
Later reviews found underground workings, yet no artifact proves a vault or its builders.
Proven: excavations and flooding are recorded. Probable: some features were engineered. Unknown: contents, makers, and remaining treasure.
The strongest find may be the persistence of the search, not its prize.
