A dead man was found on a beach with every label cut from his clothes, and a secret code.
Adelaide, 1948. No wallet. No name. No cause of death.
In a hidden pocket, two words: Tamam Shud. It means, it is ended.
They were torn from a book of Persian poetry. Inside that book, a code nobody has cracked.
In 2022, DNA suggested his name was Carl Webb.
