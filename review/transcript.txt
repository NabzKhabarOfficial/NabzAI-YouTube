His last radio words were: it is not an aircraft.
Australia, 1978. Frederick Valentich was flying alone across Bass Strait. Then he called the tower about something strange.
He said a long object with bright lights was circling just above him.
The controller had no other traffic there. Then Valentich reported that his engine was running rough.
Seventeen seconds of metallic noise followed. Proven: the plane vanished. Probable: an accident. Unknown: what he saw.
Those last words still stand: not an aircraft, and no aircraft ever found.
