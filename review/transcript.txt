A room built as a royal gift survived centuries, then vanished inside a war.
What happened to the original Amber Room after Nazi forces removed it in 1941?
Panels went into 27 crates, were displayed in Königsberg, and were last documented in January 1945.
Bombing and Soviet assault destroyed the castle; searches found rumors and fragments.
Proven: the original disappeared in World War II; reconstruction opened in 2003. Probable: fire destroyed it. Unknown: surviving panels.
The room can be visited today, but its glittering certainty is a careful reconstruction, not the missing original.
