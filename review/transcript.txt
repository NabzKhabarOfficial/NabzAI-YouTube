A man in a rubber mask hijacked live TV, and was never caught.
Chicago, 1987. In the middle of a broadcast, a masked face took over. Who was he?
This needed equipment and know-how. Maybe someone who knew television from the inside.
Two hours later, he hit a second station. This was no accident; it was a plan.
Proven: a stronger signal overpowered the station links. Probable: people with broadcast skills. Unknown: who they were.
We know how he did it, just not who he was. And he never came back.
