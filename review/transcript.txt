Hunter-gatherers built monumental stone enclosures before farming became normal.
Why did people raise massive T-shaped pillars at Göbekli Tepe around 9600 BCE?
UNESCO describes round and oval structures, animal reliefs, and pillars up to about 5.5 meters tall.
The site challenged the idea that agriculture had to come before organized ritual building.
Proven: hunter-gatherer communities built the monuments between roughly 9600 and 8200 BCE. Probable: rituals and communal gatherings mattered. Unknown: their exact beliefs and purpose.
The older mystery is not lost technology, but how mobile people organized to build together.
