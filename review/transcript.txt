A man hijacked a plane, jumped out with the cash, and vanished.
1971. A man in a dark suit handed a flight attendant a note: I have a bomb.
He got two hundred thousand dollars and four parachutes, then jumped into the rainy night.
But nine years later, a boy found bundles of that same cash buried in a riverbank.
The FBI searched for forty-five years, then put the case aside.
The money was by the river. So where did he go?
