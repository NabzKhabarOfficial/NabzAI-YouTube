Desert lights seem to hover alone, but many begin with something ordinary: a car.
Why do lights near Marfa appear to drift, brighten, split, and disappear?
A 2004 study matched lights with headlights on distant U.S. 67 using cameras and traffic data.
Atmospheric layers can bend and distort those headlights until a road source looks like a floating orb.
Proven: many recorded lights matched traffic. Probable: refraction explains most modern sightings. Unknown: older and unusual reports.
The strange part may not be the source, but how far desert air can bend an everyday journey.
