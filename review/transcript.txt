Two children were remembered as green, but the oldest evidence is written, not photographed.
What happened when the children appeared near Woolpit in medieval Suffolk?
The chronicles describe unknown speech, strange clothes, hunger, beans, and a boy who died.
The surviving girl later described a dim land; malnutrition and foreign refugee children offer more ordinary possibilities.
Proven: two medieval chroniclers recorded the tale. Probable: some real encounter may underlie it. Unknown: the children’s origin and the meaning of ‘green.’
The story’s color may belong as much to memory and translation as to skin.
