We can sound out its words, but nobody knows what they mean.
Crete, almost four thousand years ago. The Minoans wrote in a script called Linear A. What did it say?
In 1952, Michael Ventris cracked a later script, Linear B. It was an early form of Greek.
Linear A shares many of those signs, so scholars can roughly say its words aloud.
But the sounds match no known language. We understand the numbers and a word for total. Little else.
The Minoans are not silent. We can hear them; we just cannot understand them.
