A famous pilot sounded close by on the radio, yet nobody saw her.
1937. Amelia Earhart hunted for a tiny Pacific island. Why didn't she reach it?
She radioed: we must be on you, but cannot see you, and gas is running low.
The ship heard her clearly; she could not hear the ship.
Proven: the plane vanished near Howland Island. Probable: it ran out of fuel and went down at sea. Unknown: where it lies.
That close voice was not rescue; it was someone beside her goal, unable to see it.
