Divers pulled a two-thousand-year-old computer from the bottom of the sea.
Greece, 1901. It looked like a lump of rusted rock.
Then X-rays revealed over thirty precise bronze gears inside.
But nothing this complex appears again for a thousand years.
Turn its handle, and it predicted eclipses years ahead. Its maker is still unknown.
That rusty rock was a calendar of the sky. So how was this knowledge lost?
